Hi! This is Iftekhar.

I'm a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, working with Dr. Weizi Li in the Fluidic City Lab.

My research sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning and intelligent transportation systems, where I teach AI agents to be good, proactive neighbors on the road. Currently, I'm working on using robot vehicles to coordinate mixed traffic—combining autonomous and human-driven vehicles—in large-scale urban environments.

Before coming to the US, I spent four wonderful years as a lecturer at Bangladesh University in Dhaka, Bangladesh. That experience taught me something important: I genuinely love helping people understand complex ideas. Whether it's explaining dynamic programming to undergrads or debugging reinforcement learning code, I find joy in the "aha!" moments.

My long-term goal is to blend teaching with impactful work. I believe the best work happens when you combine technical knowledge with the ability to communicate and collaborate effectively.

When I'm not debugging RL agents or writing papers, you'll find me cooking, watching movies, or creating educational content for my YouTube channel, where I discuss different computer science topics!.
