Research focus

Mixed Traffic Coordination

Learning‑based control policies that coordinate human‑driven vehicles (HDVs) and robot vehicles (RVs) at intersections and networks under uncertainty, with safety and efficiency trade‑offs.

RL‑guided dynamic routing, congestion mitigation, and neighbor‑aware coordination under partial observability, leveraging SUMO↔CARLA co‑simulation.
